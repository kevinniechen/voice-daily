Speak with more confidence in six minutes a day.

Ten Minute Voice is a voice coach that actually listens. Read a page aloud and your coach hears what a good voice teacher hears: breath that runs out before the end of a sentence, words swallowed at the back of the throat, endings that fade into vocal fry, a rushed pace, a flat delivery. Then it tells you why it happens and the exercise that fixes it.

ONE SHORT LESSON A DAY
• Warm up: body, breath, then sound
• Your focus drill, with the exact lines to say
• Read a page aloud and get timestamped notes; tap any note to hear that moment
• Shadow a professional narrator, sentence by sentence

A REAL CURRICULUM
Your coach picks your biggest habit and makes it your focus. Pass it on three different days and it moves to review, coming back after 3, 7 and 21 days until it sticks. Then the coach picks the next one.

PRACTISE ANYTHING, ANY TIME
Free play with every exercise you've been given, any page, any narrator, tongue-twisters, or your own recordings.

SEE YOURSELF IMPROVE
Every recording and every note is kept, so you can hear how far you've come. Streaks keep you going.

Your recordings are sent to an AI model to write your coaching notes. They're never sold or used for ads, and you can delete everything at any time.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Not collected: location, contacts, browsing history, identifiers for advertising.

## App Review notes
- The app records the user's voice and sends it to an AI model (Google Gemini via OpenRouter; Anthropic Claude as a fallback) to write coaching notes. Before the first recording, the user is shown who receives it and asked to agree.
- No login is needed to try it: a guest account is created on first launch. Sign in with Apple is optional, to keep progress across devices.
- Account deletion: Journey tab → "Delete my account and recordings".
- To test quickly: Today → Start lesson → Skip the warm-up → record the focus drill (read the lines shown) → a grade appears in about 30 seconds.
