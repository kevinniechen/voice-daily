Privacy

Ten Minute Voice · updated October 22, 2026

Ten Minute Voice is a voice coach. To coach you, it has to hear you, so this page explains plainly what happens to your recordings and everything else.

What we collect

  • Your recordings from lessons and practice, and the text you were reading.
  • What the coach produces from them: a transcript, pitch and loudness measurements, notes, grades and your practice plan.
  • Your progress: lessons, streaks, the words you mark, settings.
  • Account details if you sign in: your email address and an account ID from the sign-in provider (Apple or exe.dev).
  • A device cookie that keeps you signed in, including as a guest, and your IP address, used only to limit abuse (for example, how many new guest accounts one network can create in a day).

How your recordings are used

Transcription and pitch measurement run on our own server. To write your coaching notes, your recording and its transcript are sent to an AI model: Google’s Gemini, reached through OpenRouter. If Gemini is unavailable, the transcript and measurements (not the audio) are sent to Anthropic’s Claude instead. We also send short text, such as your exercise and reading passage, to Claude to choose practice lines and organise your lessons.

These providers process your data to return a response to us; we use their paid services, whose terms don’t allow them to train their models on what we send. We don’t use your recordings to train any model ourselves.

We ask for your agreement before your first recording is sent.

What we never do

  • We don’t sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don’t use your recordings for advertising.
  • We don’t use third-party ad trackers in the app.

Where it’s kept and for how long

Your data is stored on our server, hosted by exe.dev in the United States, so you can replay your recordings and see your progress. It’s kept until you delete it.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Journey and choose Delete my account. This permanently deletes your recordings, notes, lessons and progress from our server straight away. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to {{CONTACT}}.

Children

Ten Minute Voice isn’t intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.
